# Story variations (keep the game worth replaying)

Standing requirement from the playtest: the game needs many different story lines to play
through, not one story with different names. This file tracks what exists and what is wanted.

## Built so far
- Arc 1, "The Missing Pages" (Baron Grumblewick, the Storybook): the first, fully fleshed-out story. It is told three ways (the lonely Baron, the jealous Baron, the Baron under the ink's spell), with clues along the roads, and it can end by fighting or by listening.
- Arc 2, "The Dragon Who Didn't Want to Fight" (2026-10-18): shiny things vanish and the kingdom blames the dragon of Dragon's Peak. The Cloud Whistle (kept by the Mossy Golem in Gloomwood Mine) blows away the cloud that hides its cave. Told three ways (the lonely dragon, the dragon who thought they were presents, the dragon whose fire went out). New person: Wren at Pennywhistle, whom nobody believes; believing her (or, if the Baron is a friend, finding his letter at Hermit's Hill) spares a puzzle, and lets Brogan find his courage. Afterwards Tilly's bell is back and Wren and Brogan talk about their friend the dragon.
- Arc 3, "The Night of a Thousand Lanterns" (2026-10-18): the Festival of Lights, and the Bat King's bats are borrowing every lantern because they are scared of the dark. A new place, the Bat Belfry, climbed with the Starlight Jar from the Spider Caves. Told three ways (scared bats, a show-off king, bats who cannot sleep). New person: Pip, the littlest bat, hiding in Lantern Hollow. Reads Lumi's friendship, Bess's friendship and the Baron's friendship from earlier stories; afterwards the bats help Lumi light the town.
- Both come from chapter two on, so after the first adventure the next story is always a different one.
- Still to port from the old game: Ruby's quest and the lost baby dragon. Until they are, the goblin, Ruby and the shadow are unused (their art and `Who` entries are kept).

## Wanted (each is a data entry in the arc library, plus new people, places and art)
- A story where the villain is a friend in disguise and the hero must discover it.
- A story with no boss fight: a festival, a race or a rescue solved by choices and puzzles.
- A story where the baby dragon is the one who is lost.
- A story set mostly underground, or on the water, using a different kind of map.
- Seasonal stories (winter market, harvest, night of lanterns).
- Stories driven by an NPC's personal problem that grows into the main plot.
- A story that changes depending on which friends the hero made in earlier rounds.

## Rules for new stories
- Reuse the world: the same towns, shops and people, living their own lives.
- Each story brings at least one new person with a backstory and a choice that matters.
- Each story can end in more than one way, and the ending is remembered.
